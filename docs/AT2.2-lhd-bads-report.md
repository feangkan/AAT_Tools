## 1. NCC Part G7 — Livable housing design

> **G7P1 — Livable housing design.** Each sole-occupancy unit in a Class 2 building must be provided with at least one **level and step-free entrance door** from an accessible part of the floor; **internal doors and corridors** which facilitate unimpeded movement; a **sanitary compartment** that facilitates independent access and use, on the **entry level**; a **shower** that facilitates independent access and use; and walls constructed so as to facilitate **future installation of grabrails** without removing existing linings.
>
> **Applications:** G7P1(a) only applies to a sole-occupancy unit on an **accessible floor**. G7O1 / G7P1 only apply to a **Class 2 sole-occupancy unit**.
>
> **G7D2.** Each sole-occupancy unit in a Class 2 building must comply with the **ABCB Standard for Livable Housing Design**, **except for Part 1** (the street-to-door path — already covered by Part D4 and the Premises Standards).
>
> — ABCB, *NCC 2022*, Volume One, cl. G7P1, G7D2.

The course asks for Part G7 on a Class 2 building. Ours is **Class 3 lodging** (AT2.1 §1, A6G4): unrelated students, shared 120–150 m² kitchen, managed occupancy. We are **not** reclassifying to make G7 a Deemed-to-Satisfy pathway. **Clause 58** still attaches because the site is **ACZ1** (AT1.2) — that is a planning trigger, not a building-class trigger. G7 is applied as the amenity overlay the brief already asked for (“LHD where possible”) on every studio.

Every G7P1 item has a counterpart on the typical floor: lift-served corridor, no step at the unit door; studio door + ensuite door on a 6.0 m bay; ensuite on the same level; shower in that ensuite; noggings in the ensuite stud lining (not in the CLT panel). G7D2 skips LHD Part 1; our street-to-lift path is already AT2.1 §6 (AS 1428.1, **850 mm** clear, **1540 mm** turning). Parts 2–6 of the Standard are the overlay; construction numbers are on extension slides 10–11.

| Instrument | Why it attaches | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Clause 58 / BADS | Site is **ACZ1** | D18–D21 and D26–D29 on the studio module |
| NCC Part G7 | Written for **Class 2 SOUs** | Amenity overlay, not a reclassification |
| LHD Part 1 (street path) | Excluded by G7D2 | Part D4 + AS 1428.1 (AT2.1 §6) |
| LHD Parts 2–6 | G7D2 overlay | Doors, ensuite, hobless shower, noggings |

## 2. Clause 58.05-1 — Standard D18 Accessibility

> **58.05-1 Accessibility objective.** To ensure the design of dwellings meets the needs of people with **limited mobility**.
>
> **Standard D18.** At least **50 per cent of dwellings** should have: a clear opening width of at least **850 mm** at the entrance and the main bedroom; a clear path of **1.2 metres** connecting entrance, main bedroom, adaptable bathroom and living area; a main bedroom with access to an adaptable bathroom; and at least one **adaptable bathroom** meeting Design A or Design B in Table D7.
>
> — *Maribyrnong Planning Scheme*, cl. 58.05-1, Standard D18.

D18 is stricter than LHD Silver: 850 mm doors and a Table D7 bathroom in **half of all dwellings**. Our brief’s DDA ratio is **1 in 20** (5%) at 30–35 m². That 5% meets Design A/B and AS 1428.1. It does **not** meet D18’s 50%. A 15–25 m² studio cannot hold a Table D7 bathroom, a 1.2 m path and a 3.0 × 3.4 m bedroom. The response is layered: **every studio** gets the LHD overlay; **one in twenty**, on the same 6.0 m grid, is a DDA room; the **common path** (street, lobby, lift, corridor) is fully accessible. The 50% adaptable-apartment ratio is a Class 2 test this floor plate does not claim to pass.

| D18 test | Standard | This project |
|---|---|---|
| Share of dwellings | 50% adaptable | 5% DDA (brief 1:20) + 100% LHD overlay |
| Entrance / bedroom door | 850 mm | 850 mm on DDA + accessible path; 820 mm LHD on typical studios |
| Internal path | 1.2 m | 1.2 m in DDA rooms; 1.0 m LHD in typical studios |
| Adaptable bathroom | Table D7 Design A or B | DDA ensuites only |

## 3. Clause 58.05-2 — Standard D19 Building entry and circulation

> **58.05-2 Building entry and circulation objectives.** To provide each dwelling and building with its own **sense of identity**. To ensure **safe, functional and efficient movement** of residents. To ensure internal communal areas have **daylight and natural ventilation**.
>
> **Standard D19.** Entries should be **visible and easily identifiable**, and provide **shelter**, personal address and a **transitional space**. The layout should **clearly distinguish** residential and non-residential entrances; provide **windows to building entrances and lift areas**; provide visible, safe stairs from the entry level; and provide corridors with **natural light and ventilation**, no obstruction from services, and **clear sight lines**.
>
> — *Maribyrnong Planning Scheme*, cl. 58.05-2, Standard D19.

The same circulation AT2.1 measured for escape, now read as amenity. Two identities at the street: **Class 6 retail** on Nicholson Street, and a separate **residential lobby**. D19 is the planning reason those doors are not interchangeable. The two fire-isolated stairs in the RC core should be **visible from the entry level** so residents use them — a glazed screen or daylit lift lobby. The corridor is 1.8–2.0 m, risers in the core, sight lines from the lift to both stairs (~24–30 m, inside D2D6’s 9–45 m). Mid-block daylight on a double-loaded 20-room floor is the hard D19 test: end windows, a glazed lobby, or mechanical air. A 6.0 m-grid plate will not have a window in the middle of the corridor.

| D19 item | Our response |
|---|---|
| Identifiable entry | Dedicated residential lobby, separate from retail |
| Distinguish residential / non-residential | Class 6 shopfront vs Class 3 lobby |
| Windows to entries and lift | Glazed lift lobby at the RC core |
| Visible stairs | Both core stairs readable from the lobby |
| Corridor light, air, sight lines | 1.8–2.0 m clear; risers in the core; end windows or mechanical air |

## 4. Clause 58.05-3 — Standard D20 Private open space

> **58.05-3 Private open space objective.** To provide adequate private open space for the reasonable recreation and service needs of residents.
>
> **Standard D20.** A dwelling should have at least one of: ground-level **25 m²** (dimension **3 m**); a **balcony** per Table D8; podium **15 m²** (dimension **3 m**); or roof **10 m²** (dimension **2 m**) — each with access from a living room. If a cooling or heating unit is on a balcony, add **1.5 m²**. If FFL is **40 m or more**, Table D8 does not apply if Table D9 extra internal area is provided.
>
> **Table D8 — studio / 1 bedroom, other orientation:** **8 m²**, minimum dimension **1.8 m**. North: 8 m² / 1.7 m. South: 8 m² / 1.2 m.
>
> — *Maribyrnong Planning Scheme*, cl. 58.05-3, Standard D20.

Precinct 1B is **6 storeys / 19.2 m**, under 40 m, so Table D9 internal POS is **not** available. Every studio needs a balcony. On a 6.0 m facade bay, 8 m² at 1.8 m depth is **6.0 m × 1.8 m** — one structural bay. If the condenser sits on the balcony, the area becomes **9.5 m²**; keep 1.8 m depth and find the extra 1.5 m² along the facade, or put the condenser on the roof. The brief’s **50% ground-level public open space** is communal / public, not D20 private open space — both apply.

| POS option | Minimum | Used here? |
|---|---|---|
| Ground courtyard | 25 m², dim. 3 m | Podium-edge studios only, if provided |
| Balcony, studio, other orientation | **8 m²**, dim. **1.8 m** | Typical floor — one 6.0 m bay × 1.8 m |
| + condenser on balcony | + **1.5 m²** | Enlarge, or keep condensers off the balcony |
| Internal POS substitute | Table D9 at ≥ 40 m FFL | **Not available** at 19.2 m |

## 5. Clause 58.05-4 — Standard D21 Storage

> **58.05-4 Storage objective.** To provide adequate storage facilities for each dwelling.
>
> **Standard D21.** Each dwelling should have convenient access to **usable and secure** storage. Total minimum storage (including kitchen, bathroom and bedroom storage) should meet Table D10.
>
> **Table D10 (studio):** total **8 m³**, of which at least **5 m³** is **within the dwelling**.
>
> — *Maribyrnong Planning Scheme*, cl. 58.05-4, Standard D21; Table D10 as in the *Apartment Design Guidelines for Victoria*.

Five cubic metres inside a 15–25 m² studio that also holds a bed, desk, ensuite and a 1.0 m path is tight. A 600 mm-deep wardrobe 2.1 m high × 1.8 m wide is about 2.3 m³. **In-unit storage will typically fall short of 5 m³** unless the studio is at the top of the range and the wardrobe is generous. The rest of the 8 m³ is a **lockable cage** in the basement or at ground (~3–6 m³), which the brief already allows as optional basement program. Bike storage is a separate brief item, not D21.

| Storage | Table D10 studio | Likely in 15–25 m² | Where the rest goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inside the dwelling | ≥ **5 m³** | Typically **2–4 m³** | Flag the shortfall; enlarge wardrobe at 25 m² |
| Total (in + out) | ≥ **8 m³** | — | Lockable cage, basement / ground |
| Bike storage | — | — | Secure bike store, not counted as D21 |

## 6. Clause 58.07-1 — Standard D26 Functional layout

> **58.07-1 Functional layout objective.** To ensure dwellings provide **functional areas** that meet the needs of residents.
>
> **Standard D26.** Bedrooms should meet Table D11, plus extra area for a wardrobe. Living areas (excluding dining and kitchen) should meet Table D12.
>
> **Table D11:** main bedroom **3.0 m × 3.4 m**. **Table D12:** studio / 1-bedroom living **10 m²**, minimum dimension **3.3 m**.
>
> — *Maribyrnong Planning Scheme*, cl. 58.07-1, Standard D26.

This is the sharpest conflict with the locked brief. Table D11 + Table D12 + wardrobe + ensuite is a **one-bedroom apartment**, not a 15–25 m² student studio (10.2 m² + 10 m² already, before ensuite). We are **not** enlarging the AT1 studio. What D26 still gives, as overlay, is a long bed wall on the 6.0 m bay, a clear 3.0 m to sit and work, and a wardrobe *additional* to the sleeping rectangle. Residents eat in the 120–150 m² communal kitchen, so Table D12 living area is partly provided in common space. That argues the *objective*; it does not claim the typical studio meets the apartment tables.

| D26 table | Apartment figure | Student studio (locked) | Objective still addressed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main bedroom | 3.0 × 3.4 m + wardrobe | Sleeping zone inside 15–25 m² | 6.0 m bay supplies the long dimension |
| Living (studio / 1 bed) | 10 m², dim. 3.3 m | Combined live/sleep | Communal kitchen/dining 120–150 m² |
| DDA room | Same tables + circulation | 30–35 m² | Closest to a D26-compliant SOU |

## 7. Clause 58.07-2 — Standard D27 Room depth

> **58.07-2 Room depth objective.** To allow adequate **daylight** into single-aspect habitable rooms.
>
> **Standard D27.** Single-aspect habitable rooms should not exceed a room depth of **2.5 times the ceiling height**. Open-plan depth may increase to **9 metres** if living, dining and kitchen are combined, the kitchen is furthest from the window, and ceiling height is at least **2.7 m**. Measure from the **external surface of the habitable-room window** to the rear wall.
>
> — *Maribyrnong Planning Scheme*, cl. 58.07-2, Standard D27.

This is the clause the course flags for written reflection. **2.5 × 2.7 m = 6.75 m.** A studio one structural bay deep, glass to corridor wall, sits at about **6.0 m** — inside 6.75 m, so the 9 m concession is not needed. There is no in-board kitchen: ensuite and wardrobe sit at the **corridor** (dark) end; desk and bed toward the **window**. FTC ≥ 2.7 m is a locked brief number, measured to the finished ceiling after the CLT acoustic topping (~100 mm STC 50 or ~195 mm STC 58). Services may drop below 2.7 m over the ensuite, as D27 already allows over a kitchen.

| Depth test | Limit | Typical studio on a 6.0 m grid |
|---|---|---|
| Single-aspect depth | **2.5 × FTC** = **6.75 m** at 2.7 m | ~6.0 m glass to corridor wall |
| Open-plan concession | 9 m at ≥ 2.7 m FTC | Not needed |
| Dark end of the room | Kitchen furthest from the window | Ensuite + wardrobe at the corridor |
| FTC | ≥ 2.7 m | Locked brief; confirm after acoustic buildup |

## 8. Clause 58.07-3 — Standard D28 Windows

> **58.07-3 Windows objective.** To allow adequate **daylight** into new habitable room windows.
>
> **Standard D28.** Habitable rooms should have a **window in an external wall**. A window may serve a bedroom from a smaller secondary area, clear to the sky, at least **1.2 m** wide and not deeper than **1.5 times its width**.
>
> — *Maribyrnong Planning Scheme*, cl. 58.07-3, Standard D28.
>
> **F6D7 — Natural lighting.** A habitable room must have a window or roof light with an aggregate light-transmitting area of not less than **10% of the floor area** of the room.
>
> — ABCB, *NCC 2022*, Volume One, cl. F6D7.

D28 is a yes/no test: every habitable room looks at an external wall. Each studio is a facade bay — no borrowed-light bedrooms. The 10% window-to-floor ratio is the number next to that clause: **2.0 m²** of glass on a 20 m² studio, **2.5 m²** on 25 m². On a 6.0 m bay with 2.7 m FTC, a sliding door 2.4 m × 2.1 m is 5.0 m² — also the D20 balcony door. A 1.8 × 1.2 m punched window (2.16 m²) passes 10% on 20 m² and fails on 25 m². Drawing rule: **the balcony door is the daylight window**, glass ≥ 10% of the room, on the external wall.

| Daylight test | Requirement | Typical 20 m² studio |
|---|---|---|
| D28 — window in an external wall | Yes / no | Yes — one 6.0 m facade bay |
| F6D7 — glass ≥ 10% floor | 2.0 m² (20 m² room); 2.5 m² (25 m²) | Balcony door ~2.4 × 2.1 m = 5.0 m² |
| Secondary daylight area | 1.2 m wide, depth ≤ 1.5 × width | Not required if glass is on the facade |

## 9. Clause 58.07-4 — Standard D29 Natural ventilation

> **58.07-4 Natural ventilation objectives.** To encourage **natural ventilation** of dwellings and allow occupants to manage it.
>
> **Standard D29.** Maximise **openable windows, doors or other ventilation devices** in external walls. At least **40 per cent of dwellings** should provide effective **cross ventilation** with a breeze path of **5–18 m** between openings of approximately the same area, on **different orientations**.
>
> — *Maribyrnong Planning Scheme*, cl. 58.07-4, Standard D29.

A double-loaded corridor on a 6.0 m grid produces **single-aspect** studios. That plate cannot put 40% of rooms on two orientations without breaking the AT1 typical floor. Four corners on a 20-room floor is 20%, not 40%. Remaining rooms ventilate through the openable balcony door and, where the brief allows it, **air-conditioning** (AT2.3, J6). The objective — occupants able to manage ventilation — is still met by a full-height openable door on every studio. Cross-ventilation that *does* exist sits in **communal** rooms (kitchen/dining, end lounges), where students spend waking hours, even though those rooms are not in the 40% dwelling count.

| Ventilation | D29 | This typical floor |
|---|---|---|
| Openable device in an external wall | Maximise | Balcony sliding door to every studio |
| Dual-aspect / cross-vent dwellings | ≥ **40%**, breeze path 5–18 m | Corner rooms only — **below 40%**; stated as a variation |
| Mechanical fallback | Brief allows AC | Per-room AC; see AT2.3 J6D3 / J6D4 |
| Communal cross-vent | (not in the 40% count) | Kitchen/dining and end lounges |

## 10. LHD doors, corridors and thresholds

> **Clause 2.1 / 3.1.** Entrance and internal doors: minimum clear opening **820 mm** (typically an **870 mm** leaf).
>
> **Clause 3.3.** Corridors connected to those doors: minimum clear width **1000 mm**.
>
> **Clause 2.2 / 3.2.** Threshold **level**, or sill **≤ 5 mm** if rounded or bevelled, or ramped not steeper than **1:8**.
>
> — ABCB, *Livable Housing Design Standard 2022* (v1.3), Parts 2 and 3.

This is the door-width reflection the course asks for. LHD Silver is 820 mm. On the **accessible path and every DDA studio**, AT2.1 already uses the stricter AS 1428.1 **850 mm** — wider than LHD, not narrower. Standard studios: 820 mm at unit door and ensuite door, flush or ≤ 5 mm sill into the CLT acoustic topping. There is no house-style hallway inside a 15–25 m² studio. The 1000 mm test lands on the **common corridor** (already 1.8–2.0 m) and the **clear strip past bed and wardrobe** to the ensuite, which a 6.0 m bay can hold without enlarging the AT1 module.

| Door / path | LHD minimum | What we provide |
|---|---|---|
| Studio entrance, standard | 820 mm clear | 820 mm (870 mm leaf) |
| DDA / accessible path | 820 mm LHD / **850 mm** AS 1428.1 | **850 mm** — the stricter figure |
| Ensuite door | 820 mm | 820 mm standard; 850 mm in DDA rooms |
| Threshold | Level, ≤ 5 mm, or 1:8 | Flush / ≤ 5 mm into the acoustic topping |
| Common corridor | 1000 mm | 1.8–2.0 m (AT2.1 escape width) |
| Path to ensuite | 1000 mm | Clear strip past bed / wardrobe |

## 11. LHD sanitary, hobless shower and noggings

> **Clause 4.2.** Sanitary compartment: **900 mm** between walls either side of the pan; basin **≥ 450 mm** from the pan centreline; clear **1200 mm × 900 mm** in front of the pan, **clear of the door swing**.
>
> **Clause 5.2.** At least one shower **hobless and step-free**; lip **≤ 5 mm**.
>
> **Clause 6.2.** Wall reinforcing: **12 mm structural plywood** or **timber noggings ≥ 25 mm**, typically **600–1600 mm** above finished floor — unless the wall is concrete or masonry.
>
> — ABCB, *Livable Housing Design Standard 2022* (v1.3), Parts 4–6.

Every studio is single-level, so the WC in the ensuite meets “entry level.” LHD circulation is smaller than AS 1428.1’s 1540 mm turning circle — that is why DDA rooms are 30–35 m². Standard ensuites use a cavity slider or outward door so the leaf does not eat the 1200 × 900 mm zone. Hobless and step-free are both required. DDA ensuites run the AT2.1 Spec 26 membrane across the whole floor; standard studios repeat the same hobless detail. Ensuite walls are stud lining on CLT, **not** masonry, so noggings are required. Grabrails are **not** fitted now — only blocking.

| Element | LHD Silver (every studio) | AS 1428.1 (DDA only) |
|---|---|---|
| Clear zone in front of pan | 1200 × 900 mm, clear of the door | 1540 mm turning circle |
| Shower | Hobless **and** step-free; lip ≤ 5 mm | Same, full-floor membrane |
| Noggings | 12 mm plywood or 25 mm, ~600–1600 mm AFFL | Same stud walls; exemption does not apply |

## 13. Typical studio on a 6.0 m bay

This slide puts D20, D21, D26, D27, D28, D29 and LHD doors on **one room**, the locked 15–25 m² studio. No new module.

Take a mid-range **20 m²** studio on a **6.0 m** facade bay, FTC **2.7 m**, ensuite at the corridor:

- **Depth (D27):** glass outside face to corridor wall ≈ **6.0 m** < 6.75 m (2.5 × 2.7).
- **Window (D28 / F6D7):** balcony sliding door 2.4 × 2.1 m = **5.0 m²** ≥ 10% of 20 m² (need 2.0 m²).
- **Balcony (D20):** 6.0 × 1.8 m = **10.8 m²** ≥ 8 m²; if the condenser sits here, still ≥ 9.5 m².
- **Doors (LHD / D18 / AS 1428.1):** unit and ensuite **820 mm** clear on a typical room; path to ensuite **≥ 1000 mm** past the bed.
- **Storage (D21):** wardrobe on the corridor wall, about 2–3 m³; flag the 5 m³ in-unit shortfall; cage downstairs.
- **Ventilation (D29):** one orientation only — openable balcony door; AC as the brief allows.

The ensuite and wardrobe take the dark end; bed and desk take the window end. That is D27’s “kitchen furthest from the window” idea without a kitchen in the room.

| Check | Number on this 20 m² bay | Pass? |
|---|---|---|
| D27 depth | ~6.0 m vs 6.75 m | Yes |
| D28 / 10% glass | 5.0 m² vs 2.0 m² | Yes |
| D20 balcony | 10.8 m² vs 8 m² / 1.8 m | Yes |
| LHD door | 820 mm typical | Yes (850 mm is DDA) |
| D21 in-unit 5 m³ | ~2–3 m³ | Shortfall — cage |
| D29 40% cross-vent | Single-aspect | Variation — see slide 9 |

## 14. DDA studio at 1:20 on the same 6.0 m grid

> **Project brief.** One DDA accessible room for every 20 single units per floor, **30–35 m²**, on the same structural grid as the typical studios.
>
> **AS 1428.1.** Door clear opening **850 mm**. Wheelchair turning space **1540 mm** diameter, free of fittings.

The DDA room is not a different structure. It is **two 6.0 m bays** (or one bay plus a borrowed strip) so the 1540 mm circle fits at the bed, the wardrobe and the ensuite. Typical 20 m² cannot hold that circle and an ensuite; that is why the brief enlarges only these rooms, not every room.

On a 20-room floor that is **one DDA + nineteen typical**. Place the DDA next to the lift lobby so the accessible path from the street (AT2.1 §6) does not travel the full corridor. Ensuite: hobless shower, Spec 26 membrane across the whole floor, 850 mm door, noggings as LHD Part 6. This room is also the D18 Table D7 adaptable bathroom — Design A, because AS 1428.1 already demands the larger circulation. It does not make the other nineteen rooms into D18’s 50%.

| Piece | Typical studio | DDA studio |
|---|---|---|
| Area | 15–25 m² | **30–35 m²** |
| Grid | One 6.0 m bay | Same grid, more of it |
| Door | 820 mm LHD | **850 mm** AS 1428.1 |
| Turning | 1000 mm path to ensuite | **1540 mm** circle in room and ensuite |
| Count per floor | 19 of 20 | **1 of 20** |
| Location | Along the corridor | Adjacent to the lift lobby |

## 15. Door-width stack: LHD, D18, AS 1428.1

Three different instruments name three different door numbers. Putting them on one slide stops the folio from mixing them.

| Instrument | Clear opening | Where it applies on this project |
|---|---|---|
| LHD Standard cl. 2.1 / 3.1 | **820 mm** (870 mm leaf) | Every typical studio door and ensuite |
| Clause 58 D18 | **850 mm** at entrance and main bedroom | Written for 50% of *apartments*; we apply 850 mm on DDA rooms only |
| AS 1428.1 | **850 mm** on a continuous accessible path | Street, lobby, lift, DDA door, DDA ensuite |
| LHD corridor cl. 3.3 | **1000 mm** | Path to ensuite; common corridor is already 1.8–2.0 m |
| D18 internal path | **1.2 m** | DDA rooms; not claimed on 15–25 m² studios |
| AS 1428.1 turning | **1540 mm** diameter | DDA rooms and accessible sanitary |

The strictest number on the **accessible path** is 850 mm, so that path is drawn at 850 mm, not 820 mm. Typical rooms can stay at LHD 820 mm without pretending they are D18 apartments. Never label an 820 mm typical-studio door as “AS 1428.1 compliant.”

## 16. Communal kitchen as the D26 living room

> **Standard D26 / Table D12.** Studio living area **10 m²**, minimum dimension **3.3 m**, excluding dining and kitchen.
>
> **Project brief.** Main kitchen and dining **120–150 m²**; level lounges **20 m²**; common study **100 m²**.

D26’s living-room table does not fit inside 15–25 m². The Class 3 answer is that **living happens in common space**. The 120–150 m² kitchen/dining is the building’s living room: it has the 3.3 m dimension many times over, daylight from an external wall (D28), and can be dual-aspect for D29 even when studios are not. Level lounges at 20 m² sit on corners where possible. Bookable study rooms at 10 m² each match Table D12’s area, used as shared desks rather than private living rooms.

This does not delete D26 from the studio. It locates the missing 10 m² where a lodging building actually puts it, which is the honest argument to 58.07-1’s *objective* (“functional areas that meet the needs of residents”) without redrawing the AT1 module as apartments.

| D26 living demand | In the studio | In common space |
|---|---|---|
| 10 m² living, dim. 3.3 m | Combined live/sleep only | Kitchen/dining 120–150 m² |
| Daylight to that living area | Balcony door (D28) | External-wall kitchen / lounge |
| Cross-ventilation | Single-aspect variation | Dual-aspect kitchen / end lounges |
| DDA equivalent | 30–35 m² room | Same common rooms, accessible path |
