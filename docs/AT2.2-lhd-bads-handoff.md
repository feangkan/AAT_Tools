## Numbers already in the repo (verify against live Clause 58)

From `data/rules/compliance_rules.json` and curated excerpts:

- Studio 15–25 m² (brief) — Clause 58 dwelling-size tables may be **larger**; flag any conflict honestly
- Window ≥ **10%** of floor area
- Cross ventilation **40%** of dwellings where practicable
- Private open space: studio balcony often **8 m²**, min dimension **1.8 m** (other orientations); +1.5 m² if AC sits on the balcony
- At **≥ 40 m** height, balcony may be replaced with extra internal living area (8 m² studio) — only relevant if the tower is that tall (Precinct 1B is 19.2 m, so **balconies stay**)
- DDA 1:20 per floor (brief)
