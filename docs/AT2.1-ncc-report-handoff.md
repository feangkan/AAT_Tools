### Sheet 05 — Wet areas 10.2 → F2 (continues AT1.4 Sheets 07 + 20)

Waterproofing sits on the **CLT acoustic buildup**, not on raw CLT. AT1.4 Sheet 07: finish + screed + resilient mat + CLT; STC 50 ≈ 100 mm, STC 58 ≈ 195 mm. Membrane and falls live in that topping. DDA shower is hobless (AS 1428.1) so set-down is in the same buildup. Flanking break at **CLT-to-RC core** (Sheet 07) is a wet-area / acoustic junction, not only a connector.

**Diagram:** Ensuite cut through CLT — tile, membrane, screed, mat, CLT, exposed soffit (char-design from Sheet 19).
