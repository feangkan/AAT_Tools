## Overview

This week's lecture covers PBSA (Purpose-Built Student Accommodation) as an operational and financial system, not just a building typology. Content directly applies to **AT1.4: Mixed-Use Development at 63–67 Nicholson St, Footscray**.

## Key Topics

- **PBSA Typology**: Hybrid of apartment, hotel, and residential college models
- **Five Community Models**: Staircase clusters, pavilion courts, mixed-type, vertical communities, distributed amenity
- **Operational Metrics**: Efficiency (70–80% net), circulation (20–30%)
- **The Corridor Problem**: Double-loaded corridors are efficient but sterile
- **Architectural Agency**: Driving industrialized systems with skill, not resisting them
- **Structural Longevity**: Designing for adaptability, disassembly, and material reuse

## Best For

- Designing community clusters in residential schemes
- Understanding developer/financial constraints and opportunities
- Justifying efficiency trade-offs in your design documentation
- Researching modular construction and mass timber applications
- Planning for post-occupancy adaptability
